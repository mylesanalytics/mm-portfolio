
## Data Scientist, Basketball Operations

**Company:** Los Angeles Sparks  
**Seniority Level:** Mid-Level / Manager (2+ years of experience)

### Required Skills
- Strong proficiency in R or Python and SQL.
- Experience building statistical and machine learning models using real-world datasets.
- Ability to communicate complex analytical findings to coaches, scouts, and other non-technical audiences.
- Experience creating data visualizations using tools such as ggplot2, Plotly, Shiny, or Dash.
- Understanding of basketball analytics, advanced metrics, and player performance evaluation.
- Ability to work flexible hours during the WNBA season.

### Preferred Skills
- Experience working with basketball tracking data, video logs, and advanced play-by-play datasets.
- Knowledge of Bayesian modeling, optimization techniques, and time-series forecasting.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure.
- Experience developing ETL pipelines and working with software engineering tools.
- Previous experience in professional or collegiate sports analytics.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative field (listed as preferred).

## Analyst, International Baseball Strategy & Information

**Company:** Los Angeles Dodgers  
**Seniority Level:** Entry-Level / Early Career (0-3 years of experience)

### Required Skills
- Ability to write production-quality SQL and Python or R code.
- Strong quantitative reasoning, statistics, and data analysis skills.
- Ability to communicate technical findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Ability to work with ambiguous problems and develop practical solutions.
- Understanding of baseball and international scouting markets.
- Relevant bachelor's degree and ability to work a varied schedule.

### Preferred Skills
- Experience building data pipelines and using ETL or dbt workflows.
- Fluency in Spanish or Japanese.
- Experience working with geographically distributed or multiple data sources.
- Familiarity with cloud infrastructure such as AWS.
- Experience with business intelligence tools and dashboards.

## Skills That Appeared in Both Job Postings
- **Programming:** R or Python and SQL.
- **Statistical Analysis:** Using data to identify patterns and support better decisions.
- **Communication:** Translating complex data into information that coaches, scouts, and leadership can understand.
- **Problem Solving:** Applying analytical methods to real-world sports problems.
- **Data Management:** Working with complex datasets and maintaining reliable information.
- **Collaboration:** Working with different departments to support organizational decisions.

## Personal Reflection

What stood out to me most was how both positions emphasized the importance of communication alongside technical skills. I think it's interesting because you can build an incredible model, but if you can't communicate those findings in a way that coaches or decision makers can understand and actually apply, then how much value is that model really providing? I also found it interesting that the Dodgers position is more focused on building data infrastructure while the Sparks position focuses more on predictive modeling and player evaluation. My ultimate goal is to work in basketball analytics and develop models that can potentially change how the game is analyzed and played, so seeing the different skillsets required gives me a better understanding of what I need to focus on throughout my education.
